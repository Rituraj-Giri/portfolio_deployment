01 / ABOUT

Design student turning briefs into posters, mockups & brand ideas — with type, texture and a growing AI-assisted workflow.

I'm a graphic design student at Patan College for Professional Studies, building a practice around branding, poster design and digital illustration. Most of what I make starts as a hackathon brief or a personal one I set myself — event posters, place-branding, product mockups.

I'm also folding AI-assisted workflows into how I design, using them to move faster from concept to a finished, print-ready poster without losing the hand-built, collage feel I like working in.
